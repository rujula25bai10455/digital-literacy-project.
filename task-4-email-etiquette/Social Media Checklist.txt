Social Media Checklist: 5 Do’s and 5 Don’ts
5 Do’s for College Students:
* Do use privacy settings to control who sees your personal posts .
* Do think critically before sharing or liking news to avoid spreading misinformation.
* Do keep your LinkedIn profile updated with your latest academic achievements.
* Do engage in respectful and constructive discussions in academic or professional groups.
* Do use a professional-looking handle or username for platforms like GitHub and LinkedIn.
5 Don’ts for College Students:
* Don’t  post offensive, discriminatory, or unprofessional content that could alert future employers.
* Don’t share sensitive personal information like your home address or phone number publicly.
* Don’t engage in or encourage cyberbullying or online harassment.
* Don’t tag others in photos or posts without their explicit permission.
* Don’t click on suspicious links shared via social media messages that might lead to phishing sites.
